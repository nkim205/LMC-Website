The Problem

After a catered event, leftover food is only useful for a short window. If it takes too long to report, or students can’t quickly tell what’s available and where, good food still ends up in the trash.

Slow Reporting

Organizers are busy wrapping up. If posting leftovers feels like a chore, it gets skipped.

Unclear Alerts

Missing details like location, amount, or deadline make it hard to decide fast enough to go.

Limited Tracking

Without simple data, it’s hard to see which events, places, and times work best.

Meet Green Forks Connect

We build on the system students already use, in three simple steps. Tap a step to see it.

Organizers report leftovers in about a minute.

A short mobile form asks only for what students need to know:

  • Event or organization name
  • Pickup location
  • What food is available
  • Estimated servings
  • Pickup deadline
  • Dietary or allergen notes, when known

Students get one clear alert they can act on.

Every report becomes a consistent message, so you can decide in seconds:

  • What food is available
  • Where to go
  • How much there is
  • When to get there by

Pickup windows are short (about 15 to 20 minutes), so clarity matters.

Green Forks sees what’s working.

The same report feeds a simple dashboard for the Green Forks team:

  • Events and meals shared
  • Popular pickup locations
  • Trends over time
  • Repeat participants

This is an internal tool for administrators. Students don’t need to log in.

Why It Matters

Less Food Waste

More leftovers reach hungry students instead of the bin.

Faster Sharing

Quicker reports and clearer alerts fit the short pickup window.

Better Data

Simple tracking shows what’s working so the program can grow smartly.
